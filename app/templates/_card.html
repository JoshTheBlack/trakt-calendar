WHEN TWO SERVICES DESCRIBED THE SAME AIRING, this card is the one place that shows. `item.alternatives` is {field: {service: value}} for the fields they filled in DIFFERENTLY — and only those, so a field they agreed on draws nothing and a card only one service described carries none of this at all. A DUAL CHIP, in the meta row, for the short scalar values where showing both is cheaper than making anybody click.
