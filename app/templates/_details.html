{# WHAT A TITLE IS, DRAWN ONCE. The calendar, the tracker and the public share page each built this panel in JavaScript, from the same payload, and the three copies had drifted twice over: a certification chip existed on two of them and an IMDb rating on one. Each drift arrived the ordinary way — a field added to whichever surface was being worked on — so the fix is one template rather than three kept in step, and the field set here is the UNION of what the three drew. TWO AXES OF VARIATION, AND NOTHING ELSE. `poster` — the picture the CARD was drawn with, where the surface has a card. Empty is not "no picture": the answer carries the describing service's own and the hero falls back to it, so every surface draws one. `progress` — this viewer's own record of the season, or nothing. Given one, every episode row grows a tick per service the account syncs, the heading grows a count and the two services are named wherever they disagree. Given none, the rows are the plain num/title/rating/date they always were. The TEMPLATE renders ticks if it is handed them; the ROUTE decides whether to look — so a surface opting in is one declared read rather than a template that knows the tracker exists. Context it reads: `d` (one source's detail answer, whose key set is the same whoever gave it — see DetailPort in app/providers/base.py), `media`, `season` (the season the rows below it are), `title` (the card's own, for the poster's alt text), `poster` and `progress`. #} {% import '_source_logo.html' as src with context %} {# ONE TICK.
