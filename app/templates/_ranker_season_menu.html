Asked for when the row's "Whole show or season…" is opened (_ranker_move_menu.html) and drawn from app/ranker/seasons.py's options. THE MOVE MENU'S OWN SHAPE: one form carrying the address, the encoder and the row's key; each choice is only its own `name="season"` and value, which the browser adds to the form's fields when that button is the one pressed. An empty value is the whole show. The answer is the page, with the row under its new key in the place it already had. HELD CHOICES ARE DISABLED, NOT LEFT OUT — including what this row is now — so the list still reads as the show's seasons, says which of them the board already has, and can never make a second row for one of them. A disabled button is never a form's submitter, so nothing here has to refuse one. THE SUGGESTION IS SAID IN WORDS beside its choice rather than only coloured, and it is never a season that has not aired yet — those are listed, dated, and can still be chosen. #}
