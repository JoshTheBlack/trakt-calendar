{# THE BULK MOVE IS A FORM, AND THE SELECTION IS ITS FIELDS. Every row, in the pool or a tier, carries a hidden field named `pick:` that is enabled exactly while the drag library has that row picked (_ranker_row.html). Move submits the enabled ones plus the chosen destination; Clear is the form's reset button. AN EMPTY FORM ELEMENT, WITH THE CONTROLS ASSOCIATED BY `form=`, rather than one wrapped round the rows. A row's own ✕ posts itself, and htmx sends the enclosing form's fields with any request from inside it — so wrapping the rows would have put the whole selection in the body of every removal. Association reaches exactly the controls named, wherever they sit. AFTER BOTH COLUMNS, and that is what makes the count free: a CSS counter reads whatever has been counted BEFORE it in the document, and a pick can be in either column.
