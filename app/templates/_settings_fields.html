The panel holds controls that are not settings — the encryption section, the Trakt pairing, the arr loaders — and htmx sends an enclosing form's fields with any request made from inside it, so wrapping the panel in the form would put every typed credential into the body of every one of those presses. The form is an empty element and the fields point at it: the shape the rankings board's selection form already takes. A CREDENTIAL IS NEVER DRAWN BACK, not masked, not its length. Its field arrives EMPTY, says whether a value is stored, and — only when one is — offers a box to clear it. What is typed replaces it, and an empty field leaves it alone; that rule is app/config.py's `apply_form`, which is also where the clear box's name is spelled (`secrets[name].clear_name`).
