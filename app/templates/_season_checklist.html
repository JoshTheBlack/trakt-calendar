What a surface decides is what submitting it DOES — the form's own attributes (`form`, rendered by Jinja's `xmlattr`, which escapes every value): a json-enc post that adds, or one that jumps. What it offers, greys and suggests is app/providers/season_choices.py's, one rule for all three. ONE FORM, ONE SUBMIT, NAMING THE RESULT BY REFERENCE. The hidden fields are the reference the result was drawn with (`ref`) plus anything the surface's route always needs (`hidden`); each ticked season adds its own field, `season:` (season_choices.FIELD_PREFIX — one flat field per season, because json-enc turns a repeated name into a list only when there is more than one of it). The server reads the title back out of what a service said. A SEASON THAT CANNOT BE PICKED IS LISTED AND SAYS WHY: held already (greyed, in the surface's words), refused (the calendar's filters), or — where the surface can send the viewer to it — a link to where it already is. The suggested one is said in words, and one not aired yet carries its date. `ticked` is the season to arrive ticked: a result that IS one season offers that season alone, ticked, so the answer to "which?" is already there.
