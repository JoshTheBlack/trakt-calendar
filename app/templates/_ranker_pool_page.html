THE TWO PARTS LAND IN DIFFERENT PLACES, which is why the rows travel out-of-band. The requesting sentinel is a SIBLING of #rankerPool (see _ranker_pool_sentinel.html for why), so its own outerHTML swap replaces it with the next sentinel — or, on the last page, with nothing at all. The rows have to go INSIDE the pool, and appending them is the one mutation that cannot disturb a drag in progress: it adds to the end and moves nothing already there. htmx removes this wrapper from the response once it has appended the rows, so the duplicate id never reaches the document. #}
